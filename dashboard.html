Laboratory Safety Guidelines
  • Always handle borosilicate glassware and microscopes with clean hands and stable surfaces.
  • Report any broken or damaged equipment immediately to the lab administrator.
  • Borrowed items must be returned within the assigned schedule (maximum 3 days).
  • First aid materials must be put back in their proper designated emergency stations after use.
